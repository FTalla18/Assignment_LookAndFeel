Trip style

    How we plan your trip

    1. Pick your window

      Tell us when you can travel. We show how dark each destination is that month and what the weather usually does.

    2. We watch the forecast

      In the week before you leave, we track aurora and cloud forecasts and move your night excursions to the clearest evenings.

    3. Go out when the sky opens

      Guides drive to clear skies, sometimes two hours away. If you see no aurora on an aurora trip, your next trip is 20% off.

    Flying from Florida?

    We book routings from Tampa, Sarasota and Orlando, rent the heavy parkas for you, and send a packing list written for people who own more sandals than boots.
