Hours of dark sky per night, by month

Sun at least 12° below the horizon, calculated for the 15th of each month. Click a name in the legend to hide or show it.

From April to August the sun never sinks far enough for a dark sky in Tromsø, so Sarasota actually gets more real night. That is why every aurora trip runs September to March. The Antarctic Peninsula is the mirror image: its tourist season falls in the southern summer, when darkness almost disappears.

Iceland visitors by month

Foreign visitors departing Keflavík Airport, thousands, Oct 2023 to Sep 2024. Winter months highlighted.

January had 128,100 visitors against 281,500 in August: less than half the crowds, plus 14 hours of darkness instead of about one and a half.

Antarctic Peninsula landings by season

Passengers on IAATO voyages that made landings. No tourism ran in 2020-21.

Landing passengers grew 76% between 2018-19 and 2024-25. Sites cap visitors at 100 ashore at a time, so popular departures sell out early.

Sources and method

  • Iceland monthly visitors: Icelandic Tourist Board, Tourism in Iceland in figures, October 2024. 2024 total: Keflavík Airport, 2024 passenger report.
  • Antarctica: IAATO, ATCM 46 IP104 and IAATO, ATCM 46 IP102. The 2024-25 landing figure is IAATO's preliminary estimate.
  • Temperatures: approximate 1991–2020 monthly averages from national weather services, rounded to whole degrees.
  • Darkness hours: calculated in js/data.js from each destination's latitude using the standard solar declination formula.
  • Trips, prices and bookings are fictional and exist for this class project only.
